## 1. Safety & Critical Risk School ("Cero Fatalidades")

**Objective:** Get to zero fatalities and cut LTIFR by 30% in 3 years, by making sure that every critical task is done by a certified person and that every critical control is verified.

| Program | Content | Modality | Duration | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 Corporate safety induction | Safety culture, life-saving rules, reporting, stop-work authority | Classroom + VR | 8 h | Once + annual refresher (2 h) |
| S-02 Site induction (NOM-023 for mines) | Site hazards, emergency plan, traffic rules, PPE | Classroom + field | 8–16 h | Annual |
| S-03 **Critical Risk Standards** (16 modules) | One module per critical risk: controls, the critical steps, field assessment (TD-P07) | Blended + field certification | 4–24 h each | 24 months max |
| S-04 Safety leadership for supervisors | Visible felt leadership, pre-task risk assessment, VCC, incident investigation (ICAM) | Blended cohort | 32 h | Refresher every 3 years |
| S-05 Brigades | Fire (NOM-002), first aid, confined space and heights rescue, **mine rescue** (underground) | Practical + drills | 24–80 h | Annual |
| S-06 Health | Heat stress (NOM-015), hearing conservation, ergonomics, fatigue management for 12-h shifts | E-learning + toolbox | 2–4 h | Annual |
| S-07 NOM-035 & wellbeing | Psychosocial risk factors, harassment protocol, mental health first aid for supervisors | E-learning + workshop | 2–8 h | Annual |
| S-08 Contractor Safety Leadership | For contractor supervisors: GASM standards, permits, VCC | Classroom | 16 h | 2 years |

**KPIs:** Fatalities = 0; LTIFR −30%; certified people on critical tasks = 100%; VCC findings closed ≤ 30 days.

## 2. Mining Academy

**Objective:** Safe, productive and cost-efficient extraction; build operators and technicians for open-pit and underground operations.

| Learning path | Key contents | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **Heavy mobile equipment operator** (haul truck 240 t, shovel, loader, dozer, grader) | Pre-use inspection, operation, efficient driving, dispatch system, fatigue, interaction with light vehicles | **Haul-truck and shovel simulators**, supervised OJT (80–200 h), certification |
| **Drilling & blasting** | Drill operation, blast design, explosives handling and storage (SEDENA permit), misfire management | Classroom + field; licensed blaster certification |
| **Underground operations** (Sierra Alta) | Ground control, scaling, bolting, ventilation, refuges, emergency escape | VR + practical in the training drift |
| **Processing plant** (crushing, grinding, magnetic separation, filtering, pelletizing) | Process control, control room, sampling, conveyor safety | Process simulator; OJT |
| **Mine planning & geotechnics** (professionals) | Mine design software, geotechnical monitoring, slope stability, water management | Specialized external courses + mentoring |
| **Community & environment** | Community relations, water stewardship, Mining Law obligations | Workshops |

**KPIs:** Time to competency for new haul-truck operators (from 9 to 6 months); fuel consumption per tonne-km (−3%); equipment damage incidents (−25%); tire life (+8%).

## 3. Steelmaking & Rolling Academy

**Objective:** Operate DRI, EAF, casting and rolling safely and to world-class standards; make automotive-grade quality possible.

| Learning path | Key contents | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **DRI plant** | Direct reduction process, reformer/reactor operation, gas systems, **hydrogen safety** | Process simulator + OJT |
| **EAF & ladle furnace** | Charging, scrap mix, power profile, foamy slag, refractories, ladle metallurgy, molten-metal emergencies (breakouts, water explosions) | **EAF process simulator**, VR for emergencies |
| **Continuous casting** | Tundish, mold, secondary cooling, breakout prevention, quality defects | Simulator + OJT |
| **Hot rolling** (strip and long products) | Reheating furnace, rolling, cooling, coiling, roll-shop, dimensional control | OJT + digital SOPs |
| **Overhead cranes & ladle handling** | NOM-006, rigging, signaling, ladle transport | **Crane simulator** + certification |
| **Quality — IATF 16949** | Core tools (APQP, PPAP, FMEA, SPC, MSA), 8D problem solving, internal auditor | Blended; certified external + internal |
| **Scrap & raw materials** | Scrap classification, radiation detection, quality and yield | Field + photo-recognition app |

**KPIs:** EAF availability (86% → 89%); metallic yield (+0.1 pp); customer claims (−20%); crane incidents (−30%).

## 4. Maintenance & Reliability Academy

**Objective:** Move from reactive to predictive maintenance, and build the technicians that the market does not supply.

| Level | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation (all maintainers)** | Safety in maintenance (LOTO, NOM-029, hot work), precision maintenance, lubrication, bolting, alignment | 80 h |
| **Mechanical specialist** | Hydraulics, pneumatics, gearboxes, bearings, pumps, conveyors, welding (NOM-027, AWS where required) | 160 h + OJT |
| **Electrical & instrumentation** | Medium/high voltage, motors, VFDs, arc flash, instrumentation, calibration | 160 h + OJT |
| **Automation** | PLC (brands used on site), SCADA/HMI, industrial networks, OT cybersecurity basics | 120 h + labs |
| **Reliability engineering** | RCM, FMEA, RCA, vibration analysis (ISO 18436 certification cat. I–II), thermography, oil analysis, CMMS | 120 h |
| **Planning & scheduling** | Work orders, planning, backlog, shutdowns | 40 h |

**KPIs:** Planned maintenance share (from 55% to 75%); MTBF on critical assets (+15%); automation vacancies filled internally (≥ 50%).

## 7. Talent Pipeline Programs

### 7.1 GASM Technical Apprenticeship (Mexican Dual Training Model)
- **Partners:** CONALEP and Universidades Tecnológicas near each site.
- **Cohort:** 60 apprentices per year (Acería 30, mines 20, Manzanillo 10), in mechatronics, electrical, industrial maintenance, and processes.
- **Model:** 2 years, 60% in the plant (with an assigned tutor) and 40% at school. Monthly stipend. Target **≥ 30% women**.
- **Result:** Hiring at graduation, with a target conversion of **≥ 75%**. This reduces dependence on the competitive nearshoring labor market.

### 7.2 Graduate Engineers Program ("Ingenieros en Desarrollo")
- 25 engineers per year (mining, metallurgy, mechanical, electrical, industrial, chemical).
- 18 months of rotation across the value chain, a mentor, a project, and a technical certification.

### 7.3 Women in Industry ("Mujeres que Forjan")
- Targeted recruitment and training for operator roles (haul-truck operators, crane operators, control rooms).
- Facilities and PPE adapted, mentoring network, sponsorship for leadership.
- Supports the NMX-R-025 certification.
- **Target:** women in operational roles from 4% to 10% in 5 years.

### 7.4 Scholarships and tuition aid
- Tuition support for technical degrees, engineering and graduate studies for employees (policy 6.6).
- A high-school completion program for operators (INEA partnership), because some workers have not finished basic education.

### 7.5 Community programs (social license)
- Technical training for young people from communities near the mines, in partnership with state employment services. They form a local recruitment pool.
