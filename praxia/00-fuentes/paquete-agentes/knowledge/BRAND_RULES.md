# Official PRAXIA brand rules
Source: PRAXIA Brand Guidelines v1.0 (2026); see master context for provenance.
Positioning: Human & AI Transformation Advisory. Tagline: Turn strategy into adoption. Enemy: Transformation Theater. Territory: The Adoption Gap. Symbol: The Axis = intersecting human and machine rings / adoption. Wordmark: Space Grotesk 700 with -3% tracking; do not reconstruct original logo from instructions; use approved artwork. Colors: Graphite #0C0D12; Ivory #F5F2EC; Indigo #5B4BFF; Violet #8B5CF6; Clay #E9663C. Headline: Space Grotesk; paragraphs: Inter; labels: Space Mono. 12-column grid, generous margin, typography-led hierarchy, restrained duotone glow instead of full-page gradient. Icon 1.4pt stroke; people in real work contexts, no fake handshake stock. Voice: declarative, high-conviction with proof; avoid synergy, journey, world-class, motivational clichés. Mark minimum 24px digital or 9mm printed; source manual says wordmark P >=22px/8mm; mark clearance at least ring width. Use true exported logos, do not manufacture exact graphic assets by text-only prompts. Claims must be supported.
