# 05 Customer Support

**Status:** proposed SOP; configure live permissions before use.

**Shared exit standard:** each stage produces handoff JSON, source references, status, acceptance criteria and named next owner. Gate failures return to previous owner.

1. **CX-01** — Intake and acknowledge without promising resolution.
2. **CX-01** — Classify urgency, impact and owner.
3. **DEL-01** — Resolve delivery issues.
4. **DEV-01** — Resolve technical issues.
5. **RISK-01** — Escalate privacy, legal or reputational impact.
6. **CX-01** — Confirm customer-approved closure.
7. **COM-01** — Update internal knowledge base.
