# 07 Internal Operations

**Status:** proposed SOP; configure live permissions before use.

**Shared exit standard:** each stage produces handoff JSON, source references, status, acceptance criteria and named next owner. Gate failures return to previous owner.

1. **OPS-01** — Collect weekly priorities and capacity.
2. **FIN-01** — Refresh forecasts / cash and margin model.
3. **HR-01** — Flag capacity or staffing constraints.
4. **COM-01** — Distribute weekly decisions and updates after approval.
5. **QA-01** — Verify documentation and SOP changes.
6. **CEO-01** — Review blockers and escalation.
7. **Founder** — Decide allocation and spending.
