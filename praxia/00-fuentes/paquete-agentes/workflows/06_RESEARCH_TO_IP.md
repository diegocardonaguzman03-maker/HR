# 06 Research To Ip

**Status:** proposed SOP; configure live permissions before use.

**Shared exit standard:** each stage produces handoff JSON, source references, status, acceptance criteria and named next owner. Gate failures return to previous owner.

1. **STR-01** — Frame business question.
2. **RES-01** — Design research and gather sources.
3. **DAT-01** — Build measure and statistical validation plan.
4. **RISK-01** — Check IP, privacy and claims.
5. **QA-01** — Peer review methods, biases and citations.
6. **CEO-01** — Propose methodology/product direction.
7. **Founder** — Approve public claims and launch.
