## 4. Quantified benefits at steady state (year 3)

The benefits are **conservative**. The share of each improvement credited to training uses Phillips isolation estimates.

| # | Benefit | Assumption | MXN M / year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fewer lost-time injuries | LTIFR 3.2 → 2.24 (−30%) ≈ 29 fewer LTIs × MXN 1.2 M average direct and indirect cost | 34.8 |
| 2 | Lower IMSS work-risk premium | −0.5 percentage points on a covered payroll base of ≈ MXN 3,570 M (the premium can fall by up to 1 point a year) | 17.9 |
| 3 | Reliability / EAF availability | +1 pp availability ≈ 22 kt of liquid steel × MXN 1,800/t contribution × 40% credited to training | 15.8 |
| 4 | Retention of technicians | Turnover of young technicians 18% → 12% ≈ 120 fewer exits × MXN 180,000 replacement cost | 21.6 |
| 5 | Haul-fleet operator efficiency | 3% fuel reduction on MXN 520 M of fuel (simulator + certification) | 15.6 |
| 6 | Bringing training in-house and consolidating vendors | Lower external rates, internal instructors | 8.0 |
| 7 | Metallic yield / quality | +0.1 pp yield ≈ 2 kt × MXN 14,000/t × 30% credited to training | 8.4 |
| | **Total quantified benefits** | | **122.1** |

**Not quantified but material:** preventing fatalities (human, legal and reputational cost that cannot be measured in money); avoided STPS fines and shutdowns; continuity of IATF customers; leadership succession; the social license of the mines; employer brand.

**Benefit ramp-up:** year 1 = 25% · year 2 = 60% · year 3 onward = 100%.

## 6. Funding and optimization options

1. **Phase the capex:** the simulators (MXN 40 M) can be leased or bought as training-as-a-service from OEMs.
2. **Tax deduction:** training costs are deductible business expenses. Confirm the tax treatment of stipends and scholarships with the Tax department.
3. **Partnerships:** share labs with CONALEP/UT (they supply equipment and the company supplies instructors), and co-finance programs with state governments.
4. **Contractors:** charge contractors for GASM certification at cost, recovering an estimated MXN 3–4 M per year.
5. **Year-1 minimum-viable option** (if capital is restricted): the structure + LMS + critical risk + Supervisor School for ≈ MXN 45 M of incremental opex and MXN 15 M of capex (VR + mining center upgrade). The large training center is deferred to year 2.
