# Regulatory Framework for Training in Mexico (Mining & Steel)

> **Disclaimer:** This summary supports design and planning only. Before you implement anything, have GASM's labor counsel and the Safety & Health department check every reference against the latest text published in the *Diario Oficial de la Federación* (DOF) and current STPS guidance. Standards, guidelines and fine amounts (measured in UMA) are updated regularly.

## 1. Federal Labor Law (Ley Federal del Trabajo, LFT) — Title IV, Chapter III Bis: "De la productividad, formación y capacitación de los trabajadores"

| Topic | Key requirement | What GASM T&D must do |
|---|---|---|
| Right and obligation (Art. 153-A) | Every employer must give its workers training (*capacitación y adiestramiento*) that raises their standard of living, competence and productivity, according to plans and programs agreed with the union or the workers. | Keep a formal, documented **annual Training Plan & Programs** for every site. |
| Delivery during working hours (Art. 153-A) | Training is given during working hours unless the parties agree otherwise. | Schedule training inside the shift roster. Where the CCT allows training outside hours, pay it as agreed. |
| Joint Training Commission (Art. 153-E) | Companies with more than 50 workers must set up a **Comisión Mixta de Capacitación, Adiestramiento y Productividad (CMCAP)**, with equal numbers of worker and employer representatives. | Set up one CMCAP per workplace with session minutes. It meets at least quarterly (see process TD-P11). |
| Worker obligations (Art. 153-H) | Workers must attend sessions, follow instructions and take the assessments. | Include attendance and assessment rules in internal work regulations (*Reglamento Interior de Trabajo*) and in the policy. |
| Proficiency exam (Art. 153-U) | A worker who says they already have the knowledge may prove it with documents or by passing a proficiency exam. | Offer a **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)** route in the certification process. |
| Certificates (Art. 153-V) | Workers who pass receive a **certificate of competence or skills (constancia de competencias o habilidades laborales)**. The employer reports the list of certificates issued to the STPS. | Issue a DC-3 for each completed course and report DC-4 lists (process TD-P09). |
| Seniority-based promotion (*escalafón*) (Arts. 154–159) | Permanent vacancies are filled from the category immediately below, taking into account training, seniority, demonstrated aptitude and family obligations, as regulated by the CCT. | Build **competency-based progression paths** that fit the CCT escalafón, so that training makes workers eligible for promotion. |
| Sanctions (Art. 994) | Failing to meet the training obligations is fined in UMA (roughly 50 to 5,000 UMA per violation in the current text; verify the amount). | Keep evidence ready for inspection. Run a self-audit every quarter. |

### STPS formats and systems (verify the current version of the guidelines)

| Format | Purpose | Practice at GASM |
|---|---|---|
| **DC-2** | Training Plan & Programs | Prepared each year per site, signed by the CMCAP and filed for inspection |
| **DC-3** | *Constancia de competencias o habilidades laborales*, the individual certificate | Issued within 10 business days after an approved course. Signed by the instructor and by the CMCAP worker and employer representatives |
| **DC-4** | List of certificates issued | Reported through the STPS electronic platform (**SIRCE**) |
| **DC-5** | Registration of external training agents | Every external vendor must show its STPS registration before a purchase order is released |

### Related legal regimes

| Regime | Relevance to T&D |
|---|---|
| **Ley Federal de Armas de Fuego y Explosivos** (SEDENA permits) | Blasters (*poblero/dinamitero*) must be trained and authorized; explosives handling must be controlled. |
| **Ley Minera** (reformed 2023) and Mining Law regulations | Safety obligations of concessionaires, emphasis on community relations and water. Training contributes to showing compliance. |
| **Subcontracting reform 2021 (LFT Arts. 12–15, REPSE)** | Specialized-services contractors must be registered. GASM is **jointly liable** with contractors, so it must verify their workers' competence and DC-3s. |
| **IMSS — Ley del Seguro Social, work-risk premium** | Lower accident frequency and severity reduce the annual work-risk premium (*prima de riesgo de trabajo*). This is a direct financial benefit of safety training. |
| **NMX-R-025-SCFI-2015** (Labor Equality and Non-Discrimination) | A voluntary certification that GASM targets. It requires inclusive training and equal access to development. |
| **LFT Art. 132, fr. XXXI** and the NOM-035 harassment protocol | Training on the protocol for preventing sexual harassment and discrimination. |
| **Federal Personal Data Protection Law** (2025 version) | Privacy notice and personal data controls in the LMS and in training records (CURP, assessments). |

## 3. Competency certification system — CONOCER

The **Consejo Nacional de Normalización y Certificación de Competencias Laborales (CONOCER)**, part of the SEP, publishes national **Competency Standards (Estándares de Competencia, EC)** in the RENEC registry. Certification is carried out by accredited Certification and Evaluation Bodies (**ECE**) and Evaluation Centers (**CE**).

| Use at GASM | Standard (example) |
|---|---|
| Certify internal instructors | **EC0217.01** — Delivering human-capital training courses in person, in groups |
| Certify instructional designers | **EC0301** — Designing human-capital training courses in person, in groups |
| Certify operators and technicians | Choose ECs from RENEC for mining, metallurgy, maintenance, welding, forklift and crane operation, and similar. Where no EC exists, **sponsor the development of new sector ECs** together with CAMIMEX and CANACERO. |
| Long-term goal (year 3) | Accredit GASM Academy as an **Evaluation Center (CE)** under an ECE |
