# Corporate Policy — Training & Development

## 1. Purpose
Set the principles, responsibilities and rules for training, certification and development at Grupo Acero Sierra Madre (GASM). The policy ensures legal compliance (LFT Title IV Chapter III Bis, NOM-STPS), safe and competent operations, and the development of talent.

## 2. Scope
All GASM companies and sites, all employees (unionized and salaried), interns and apprentices. **Contractors** (REPSE specialized services) fall under sections 6.4 and 6.5.

## 3. Definitions
- **Competence:** A proven ability to apply knowledge and skills to achieve intended results (ISO 10015).
- **Critical task:** A task where a failed control could cause a fatality or a catastrophic event, as defined in the Critical Risk Standards.
- **Certification (internal):** A formal statement, after an assessment in the field or on a simulator, that a person is competent to perform a critical task on their own. It is valid for a defined period.
- **DC-3:** STPS certificate of competence or labor skills.
- **CMCAP:** Joint Commission on Training, Coaching and Productivity.
- **DNC:** Training Needs Analysis (*Diagnóstico de Necesidades de Capacitación*).

## 4. Principles
1. **No certification, no critical task.** No person, whether employee or contractor, may perform a critical task without a valid internal certification. Supervisors who assign an uncertified person to such a task commit a serious breach.
2. **Training is part of work.** Training happens during working hours (LFT Art. 153-A) unless the CCT or a written agreement says otherwise. Line managers are responsible for releasing people.
3. **Needs-based.** All training comes from the needs analysis, competency gaps, legal requirements or strategic programs. There is no ad-hoc training without justification.
4. **Measured impact.** Every program has objectives, an evaluation plan and a business owner.
5. **Equal opportunity.** Access to development is non-discriminatory (NMX-R-025), with accommodations for disabilities and specific actions for women in technical roles.
6. **Internal expertise first.** Internal certified instructors are preferred. External vendors are used where they add unique value.

## 5. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Learning Council** | Approves strategy, budget and flagship programs |
| **T&D Director** | Owns the policy; ensures compliance, quality and results |
| **Site Director** | Ensures that the site's plan is carried out and that people are released for training |
| **Line managers and supervisors** | Identify needs, release people, provide OJT, sign competence assessments, follow up on application of the learning |
| **Employees** | Attend, take part, pass assessments (Art. 153-H), apply what they learn, keep their certifications current |
| **CMCAP** | Approves plans, monitors implementation, signs DC-3s (Art. 153-E) |
| **HSE** | Defines critical controls and competence requirements for critical risks |
| **Procurement** | Buys training only through the T&D catalog and from vendors registered with the STPS (DC-5) |

## 6. Rules

### 6.1 Planning
- Each site prepares its annual DNC and DC-2 plan by **30 November**. The CMCAP approves it in **January**.
- The **minimum annual training** per person is 40 hours. The target is 56 hours on average, including OJT that is documented in the LMS.
- Mandatory legal and critical-risk training has **budget priority** and cannot be cancelled for production reasons. It can only be rescheduled, within 30 days.

### 6.2 Attendance and cancellation
- An unjustified absence is handled under the Internal Work Regulations.
- Cancellations must be made at least 72 hours before the course. Late cancellations of external courses are charged to the requesting area's cost center.

### 6.3 Evaluation and certification
- Every course has a learning assessment. The minimum passing score is **80%**, or **100% on the critical steps** of practical assessments.
- DC-3s are issued only to people who passed, within **10 business days**.
- Critical-task certifications last **24 months** at most, or less if the regulation or the risk standard requires it. They are suspended after any serious incident involving the holder until the person is re-assessed.
- Workers can prove their competence without taking the course (Art. 153-U) through **Recognition of Prior Learning**.

### 6.4 Contractors
- Before starting work, every contractor worker completes the **GASM Induction** (4 h general + site-specific module) and shows a valid DC-3 for their regulated tasks.
- Contractors who perform critical tasks must hold **GASM certification**, either assessed by GASM or by the contractor's own accredited assessors audited by GASM.
- REPSE contracts include clauses on competence, records and GASM's right to audit.

### 6.5 Visitors
- Visitors receive a short safety induction and are escorted at all times.

### 6.6 Development programs and tuition support
- Leadership and high-potential programs are open through talent reviews and by application.
- Tuition support covers up to **70%** of tuition for degrees and graduate studies related to the job, subject to performance and budget.
- **Repayment agreements:** Enforceability under Mexican labor law is limited. They may only be used for high-cost programs (> MXN 150,000), must be voluntary, in writing and proportional, and must be validated by labor counsel. The main retention strategy is career paths and recognition, not penalties.

### 6.7 Internal instructors
- Internal instructors must hold **EC0217.01** certification (or be in the process of getting it) and be approved by the Academy Board for their subject.
- Part-time SME instructors receive an **instructor allowance** per hour taught, as defined by HR policy, and formal recognition in their performance review.

### 6.8 External vendors
- Vendors must be registered with the STPS (DC-5), approved by the T&D CoE, and evaluated after each course. A vendor who scores below 4.0/5 twice is removed from the catalog.
- Vendors must provide materials in Spanish and use GASM templates for assessments and records.

### 6.9 Records and data
- The LMS is the **single source of truth** for training records. Records are kept for at least the length of employment plus 5 years, or longer where a regulation requires it.
- Personal data is handled under the applicable privacy law and GASM's privacy notice.

## 7. Non-compliance
Breaches of this policy are handled through the Internal Work Regulations, the CCT and the Code of Conduct. Assigning an uncertified person to a critical task is a **serious breach**.
