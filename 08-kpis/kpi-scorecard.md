## 2. Dashboard structure

| View | Audience | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive** | Learning Council | Business impact, compliance traffic lights, pipeline, budget, ROI |
| **Site** | Site Director, Superintendents | Plan versus actual, certifications about to expire (30/60/90 days), no-shows by area, contractor induction |
| **Supervisor** | Line managers | Their team's compliance, expiring certifications, pending OJT sign-offs, L3 follow-ups |
| **Employee** | Every worker (LMS app) | Their learning path, certifications and expiry dates, DC-3s, career path |

## 3. Data governance
- **Single source of truth:** LMS (training records); HRIS/SAP (headcount, roles); HSE system (incidents); CMMS/MES (operational KPIs).
- **Monthly data close** by the 5th business day.
- **Quality:** quarterly record audits (TD-P09); KPI definitions frozen for the year and changed only by the Learning Council.
