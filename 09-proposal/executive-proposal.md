# Executive Proposal to the Board of Directors
## Creating "Academia GASM" — Corporate Training & Development Department

**To:** Board of Directors and CEO, Grupo Acero Sierra Madre
**From:** Vice-Presidency of Human Resources
**Decision requested:** Approve the creation of the corporate T&D department, a 3-year plan, an operating budget of **MXN 139 M per year at steady state** (MXN 61 M incremental) and **MXN 134 M of capex** over 3 years.

---

### 1. The problem

- **Two fatalities in three years.** In both cases the authorizations were expired or missing. **23% of critical tasks** audited had no evidence of competence.
- **Training is fragmented and driven by compliance.** Six sites run six different systems. 70% of hours are regulatory classroom courses, and nobody measures whether people are competent.
- **Legal exposure.** Half of the Joint Training Commissions (CMCAP) required by LFT Art. 153-E have lapsed, and the DC-3 records are incomplete.
- **Talent is running out.** 41% of senior maintenance technicians can retire within 7 years. Automotive plants take 18% of young technicians every year. 17 automation vacancies have been open for more than 90 days.
- **81% of supervisors** started in the role without leadership training.

### 2. The proposal

Create **Academia GASM**: a corporate hub-and-spoke T&D department built on four pillars.

| Pillar | What it delivers |
|---|---|
| **1. Cero Fatalidades: certified competence** | 16 Critical Risk Standards; field certification with expiry dates; LMS linked to work permits and gate access; the same standard for contractors |
| **2. Technical excellence** | Mining, Steelmaking and Maintenance & Reliability Academies; simulators; 120 role competency profiles; a knowledge-capture program for retiring experts |
| **3. Leaders and pipeline** | Supervisor School for 650 supervisors; leadership programs; succession for 150 critical positions; 60 dual apprentices a year; the graduate engineers program; *Mujeres que Forjan* (women in industry) |
| **4. Compliance and impact by design** | STPS compliance (DC-2/3/4/5, SIRCE, CMCAP), CONOCER certification, Kirkpatrick/Phillips evaluation, a KPI dashboard |

**Structure:** 64 people (27 already exist and move into the new structure), plus 150 certified part-time SME instructors.

### 3. Investment and return

| | MXN M |
|---|---|
| Operating budget at steady state (year 3) | 139.0 / year (≈ 3.0% of payroll; ≈ USD 890 per employee) |
| Current training spend | 78.0 / year |
| **Incremental opex** | **61.0 / year** |
| **Capex** (training centers, simulators, VR, mobile units) | **134.0** over 3 years |
| Quantified annual benefits at steady state | **122.1 / year** (safety, IMSS premium, reliability, retention, fuel, yield, in-sourcing) |
| **Steady-state ROI** | **≈ 64%** (benefit–cost ratio 1.64) |
| Payback | ≈ 4.1 years; positive NPV from year 5 |

Even the conservative scenario (60% of benefits) roughly breaks even, **before** counting the prevention of fatalities, legal compliance and succession.

### 4. Targets at 36 months
- **Zero fatalities**; LTIFR from 3.2 to **2.24**
- **100%** of people on critical tasks certified; **zero** STPS findings on training
- EAF availability from 86% to **89%**; haul-fleet fuel **−3%**
- **56 h** of training per employee per year, 60% of it aimed at performance
- **60%** of leadership roles filled internally; **75%** of critical positions with a ready successor
- Technician turnover from 18% to **12%**; women in operations from 4% to **8%**

### 5. Implementation
- **Phase 0 (months 0–3):** Hire the T&D Director, reactivate the CMCAPs, run the compliance audit, deliver quick wins (critical-task evidence, contractor induction).
- **Phase 1 (3–12):** Structure, competency framework, critical-risk certification, LMS, instructor certification, Supervisor School.
- **Phase 2 (12–24):** Training centers, simulators, academies, apprenticeships, knowledge capture, succession.
- **Phase 3 (24–36):** Digital & Green Steel skills, ROI studies, accreditation as a CONOCER Evaluation Center, NMX-R-025.

### 6. Governance
A quarterly **Learning Council** chaired by the CEO; Academy Boards sponsored by the plant directors; a **Corporate Union–Management Training Forum**; a CMCAP at every site.

### 7. Decisions requested
1. Approve the creation of Academia GASM and its structure (64 positions, phased hiring).
2. Approve the 2027 budget: MXN 45 M incremental opex + MXN 67 M capex (year 1).
3. Approve Corporate Policy POL-RH-TD-001 (Training & Development), including the rule **"No certification, no critical task"**.
4. Instruct plant directors to include protected training time in the shift rosters and to take on the KPIs shared with T&D.
5. Authorize the start of the joint co-design with the union through the Corporate Training Forum.
