## C. General induction contents (4 h)
1. GASM's *Cero Fatalidades* commitment and life-saving rules
2. Stop-work authority and incident / near-miss reporting
3. Site hazards and critical controls
4. Permits to work and isolation
5. Traffic rules and vehicle–pedestrian interaction (mines and plants)
6. Emergencies: alarms, muster points, brigades
7. Environment: waste, spills, water
8. Respect in the workplace: Code of Conduct, harassment protocol, NOM-035
9. Test (≥ 80%)
